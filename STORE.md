## English (`en`)

**Name:** Screenshot Buddy  
**Subtitle:** Edit, redact & organize shots  
**Keywords:** screenshot,capture,annotate,blur,redact,editor,menu bar,OCR,QR  

**Description:**

Screenshot Buddy keeps every capture in a clean gallery and gives you a fast editor: draw, arrows, text, crop, blur, and black-box.

It detects likely passwords, IBANs, cards, and other sensitive patterns so you can auto-blur in one tap. Copy text with OCR, pick hex colors, and scan every QR in a shot.

Save As, overwrite, or copy back to the clipboard. Recent shots live in the menu bar. Sensitive previews stay locked until Touch ID or your password. Built for macOS accessibility.

## Japanese (`ja`)

**Name:** Screenshot Buddy  
**Subtitle:** 編集・墨消し・整理  
**Keywords:** スクリーンショット,注釈,ぼかし,墨消し,エディタ,メニューバー,OCR,QR  

**Description:**

Screenshot Buddy はすべてのショットをすっきりしたギャラリーに保存し、描画・矢印・テキスト・切り抜き・ぼかし・黒塗りができる高速エディタを提供します。
